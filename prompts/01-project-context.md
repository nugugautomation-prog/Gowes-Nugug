# 01 — Create Project Context

Gunakan seluruh hasil discovery.
Buat `docs/PROJECT-CONTEXT.md` dengan struktur:
1. Project Overview
2. Business Problem
3. Business Goal
4. Target Users
5. Current Process
6. Desired Process
7. Known Requirements
8. Business Rules
9. User Roles
10. Constraints
11. Assumptions
12. Open Questions
13. Out of Scope
14. Risks

Pisahkan CONFIRMED, ASSUMPTION, RECOMMENDATION, OPEN QUESTION.
Jangan menambah requirement tanpa persetujuan. Jangan coding.
